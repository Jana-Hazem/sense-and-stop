**Figure E2_waveforms. Waveforms of the three rat recordings (R4, R5, R6).**

All three anesthetized rats received zolpidem during the recording; cortical spreading depolarization (CSD) was evoked with KCl. Each recording has one DC electrode channel and one Doppler (blood flow) channel.

**(a)** Full recordings, one rat per row, DC (top) and Doppler (bottom), against time since the start of the recording in minutes (shared time axis). Traces are block averages at about 1 Hz of the raw signal, in ADC volts before amplifier gain; the y-axes show the 0.5 to 99.5 percentile range of the valid data, so large artifacts are off scale. Vertical dashed lines mark typed KCl applications, the red line the typed zolpidem injection, the black line the typed end note (times from the hand-typed notes, so approximate). Grey shading marks intervals excluded by the extraction (ADC saturation, settling before the amplifier adjustment, time after the end note). Each detected wave is marked at its DC peak (marker drawn slightly above the trace; off-scale peaks are drawn at the axis edge): filled circle = baseline (pre-injection) wave kept (n = 15: R4 5, R5 4, R6 6); open circle = pre-injection wave excluded (selection in docs/s1_wave_selection.csv, not a new rule); triangle = post-injection wave, a separate drug-condition group (all post-injection waves are shown, because the selection file covers pre-injection waves only).

**(b)** One representative baseline wave per rat, DC and Doppler in a window of +/- 200 s around the DC peak. Both traces are shifted so that their median over -200 to -60 s before the DC peak is zero. Dashed lines mark the DC peak and the Doppler peak (times from the waves table); the arrow shows the delay between them. **These waves are representative and were not selected for appearance.** Rule: among the kept baseline waves of a rat, the one whose FWHM is closest to the median FWHM of that rat's kept baseline waves (ties: the earlier wave). Selected: R4: wave 4 at 59.5 min (FWHM 35 s, median of kept baseline waves 35.0 s, DC to Doppler delay 61 s); R5: wave 7 at 57.1 min (FWHM 38 s, median of kept baseline waves 35.5 s, DC to Doppler delay 52 s); R6: wave 7 at 46.7 min (FWHM 56 s, median of kept baseline waves 57.0 s, DC to Doppler delay 28 s).
